2. Bank Transfer

A bank transfer is made using the payment details shown to the customer after this payment method is selected. The order is dispatched once the payment has been recorded. Enter the order number in the payment description or reference-number field.

3. Currency Conversion Statement

All payments are made in euros (EUR). If a debit or credit card is not linked to an account in euros, the card issuer converts the amount into the account currency according to its own exchange rate and rules. Due to conversion or issuer fees, the final amount charged may differ from the amount shown in euros.

4. Delivery

Delivery is available in the Republic of Croatia and in European Union countries for which the checkout offers a delivery method. Delivery outside these areas is not available without prior written agreement.

The customer may provide a delivery address different from their own address. Available methods and the final delivery cost are shown before the order is confirmed. Delivery is normally handled by BOX NOW or another contracted delivery service shown at checkout.

The usual delivery time is 3 to 5 working days within Croatia, 3 to 7 working days for Croatian islands, and 3 to 8 working days for orders received after 11:00 on Friday or during the weekend. Delivery time to other European Union countries depends on the destination.

The customer must inspect the package upon receipt. Visible damage must be reported to the delivery service immediately and, where possible, the damaged shipment should be refused.
